    How to play

    Ludo in thirty seconds

    • Get out. Roll a 6 to move a piece from your yard onto your coloured start square.
    • Go round. Travel clockwise around the track, then up your own colour lane into the centre.
    • Roll again. A 6 earns another roll — but three 6s in a row and you lose the turn.
    • Capture. Land on an opponent to send it back to their yard, and earn another roll.
    • Safe squares. Coloured start squares and starred squares can't be captured.
    • Come home. You need the exact number to step into the centre.
    • Win. The first player to bring all four pieces home wins.
